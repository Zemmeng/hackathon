PROBLEM STATEMENT

Closures disrupt drivers, public transport and pedestrians. Planners need to test the impact, including overlapping works, before deployment.

OUR INNOVATION

Four AI driver personas

Commuter / Local / Visitor / Delivery

They read the same sign differently. Their LLM interpretations inform the simulation.

VALUE & PATH TO MARKET

Help councils and contractors compare traffic impact and equipment cost before crews arrive.

Proposed model: RPM Hire pairs impact reports with hire quotes; councils use the evidence for permit review.
